Controller: Cogniosynthesis Portal Ltd, company number 16225073, registered office 15 Neptune Court, Vanguard Way, Cardiff CF24 5PJ. We are registered in England and Wales and trade as CognioNews, CognioEngine and FestivalFocus.

2. WHAT PERSONAL DATA WE COLLECT

We collect only what we need to run the service and to deal with you as a customer, reader or business contact.

  • Contact and account data: name, email address, and where relevant your organisation, role and telephone number, when you subscribe, register, submit a story or contact us.
  • Subscription and payment data: the plan you hold, its status, billing period and renewal date, and payment references from Stripe. Stripe collects and holds your card details. We do not receive or store your card number, expiry date or security code.
  • Content you give us: story submissions, comments, survey responses, emails and other messages you send us, including any files you attach.
  • Usage and device data: the pages you view on this site, the page you arrived from, approximate location (city level), device type, browser and operating system, and your IP address in our server logs.
  • Correspondence and support records: the history of your enquiries and our replies.
  • Business prospect data: see the supplementary section on B2B prospect data below.

If you subscribe to CognioNews Premium, you need to give us your email address to subscribe and sign in. Without it we cannot provide Premium. The CognioNews privacy notice describes the Premium data in full.

We do not knowingly collect special category data (for example health, ethnicity, religion, political opinions, biometric or genetic data), and we do not buy personal data from data brokers.

3. AUDITS

We carry out Epistemic Audits and AI Health Checks at your premises on our own machine, which is not connected to the internet or to your network. Your documents are loaded from your own storage, are never sent to us, and are not kept: at the end of the visit the machine is wiped clean in front of you and you receive a signed visit record of what was loaded, what was produced and when it was wiped clean. We keep one copy of the signed visit record: the date, the number of files, their digital fingerprints, the start and finish times, and the name of the person from your team who was present. It holds no file names and no document content. We keep it for six years as our business record. We do not use your documents to train any system. The written finding belongs to you.

4. HOW WE USE YOUR PERSONAL DATA

  • To provide the service: publishing content, delivering the pages you request, and managing your access and preferences.
  • To create and administer your subscription or account, and to take and manage payment.
  • To send you the newsletters, digests and service messages you have asked for, and to handle unsubscribes.
  • To answer your enquiries, deal with complaints and provide support.
  • To keep our own records, including accounting, tax and VAT records required by law.
  • To keep the service secure and available: detecting abuse, fraud, scraping, attacks and technical faults.
  • To introduce our services to named people at organisations, under the B2B section below.
  • To establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

We do not use your personal data for automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects about you, and we do not sell your personal data. Our AI analysis is applied to published news content and, during an on-site audit, to the documents a client chooses to load on our offline machine (see section 3), not to profiles of individual readers.

6. WHO WE SHARE IT WITH

We do not sell personal data. We share it only where we need to in order to run the service, and only with organisations that are bound to protect it.

  • Payment processing: Stripe Payments UK Ltd and the Stripe group. Stripe processes card data as an independent controller for payment processing and fraud prevention. We receive limited transaction data back from Stripe to reconcile your subscription.
  • Hosting and infrastructure: our hosting provider, with servers in the United Kingdom. It hosts our websites, server logs and service data.
  • Email delivery: our email provider (UK), which sends our newsletters and service emails such as receipts and sign-in links.
  • Professional advisers: our accountants, auditors and lawyers, where necessary and under duties of confidence.
  • Authorities and third parties: where we are required by law, by a court order, or where necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal claims, or to protect someone's vital interests.
  • Business transfers: if we sell or transfer all or part of the business, relevant data may be transferred to the buyer under equivalent protections.

We do not use advertising networks or social media tracking pixels on this site, and we do not share personal data for others' marketing.

7. INTERNATIONAL TRANSFERS

Our hosting provider's servers are in the United Kingdom, and our email provider is in the United Kingdom. Some of our processors are based in, or may access data from, the United States:

  • Stripe, LLC — covered by the UK–US Data Bridge (the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework). If the Data Bridge cannot be used, Stripe's Data Transfers Addendum applies the UK International Data Transfer Addendum instead.

Where we transfer personal data outside the UK, we rely on UK adequacy regulations, including the UK–US Data Bridge. If those regulations stop applying to a recipient, we will only continue the transfer under another lawful safeguard, such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum, after checking that the transfer meets the legal test for that safeguard. You can ask us for details of the safeguards used for a particular transfer.

9. YOUR RIGHTS

Under the UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • be informed about how we use your data — this notice does that;
  • access a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
  • rectify data that is inaccurate or incomplete;
  • erase your data where there is no good reason for us to keep it;
  • restrict processing while a concern is resolved;
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests, and to direct marketing at any time, absolutely;
  • data portability — receive data you gave us in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format, or have it sent to another controller where technically feasible;
  • withdraw consent at any time where we rely on consent;
  • not be subject to a decision based solely on automated processing that produces legal or similarly significant effects. We do not make such decisions about you.

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@cogniosynthesisportal.uk. We will respond within one month; if a request is complex we may extend that by up to two further months and will tell you why. Exercising your rights is free. We may ask for proof of identity so that we do not disclose data to the wrong person.

If you are unhappy with our response, you can complain to us or to the Information Commission — see section 14.
